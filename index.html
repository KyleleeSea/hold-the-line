Hold the Line

1862. You command a regiment of fifty. Form your ranks, pick your volleys, and lead the charge yourself.

Your side (the host chooses; the other player takes the opposite)
Two players: one hosts and shares the 4-letter code, the other joins. The link is peer-to-peer and works across the internet.
Commanding: pick who you are talking to with 1–5 (everyone, front rank, rear rank, 1st or 2nd section), then give the order. F follow me, H halt, G advance, Z fall back, C charge, V volley, M fire by rank, B fire at will, N hold fire, T cycle line / column / skirmish.
Fighting: click the view to take the mouse. WASD move, Shift run, click to swing the saber or fire the revolver, X switches, R reloads. A regiment breaks when it loses two thirds of its men.
