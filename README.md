# Hold the Line

A first-person American Civil War regiment commander game. You lead fifty men on foot, Mount & Blade style: give orders to the whole regiment, a single rank, or a section, and fight with saber and revolver yourself. Two-player over a direct peer-to-peer connection, or solo against an AI colonel.

Play it: open the GitHub Pages site for this repo. One player clicks **Host online battle** and shares the 4-letter code; the other joins with it.

Orders: select with 1-5 (all, front rank, rear rank, 1st section, 2nd section), then F follow me, H halt, G advance, Z fall back, C charge, V volley, M fire by rank, B fire at will, N hold fire, T formation.
